# Privacy Policy for Compass

**Last updated: July 4, 2026**

## Overview

Compass is a mental health journaling application designed to help you reflect, track patterns, and support your emotional well-being. This privacy policy explains how Compass handles your data. The short version: **we don't collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything stays on your device.**

## What Compass Does Not Do

- **No data collection.** Compass does not collect, receive, or process any personal information from you. We do not have a server, database, or any backend infrastructure.

- **No accounts.** Compass does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

- **No cookies.** Compass does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

- **No analytics or tracking.** Compass does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking services. We do not know who you are, when you use the app, or what you write.

- **No third-party services.** Compass does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or platforms. There are no external calls made from the app.

- **No data sales or sharing.** We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your data to anyone, because we never have access to it.

## Where Your Data Lives

All data you enter into Compass — your assessment answers, journal entries, mood and energy ratings, and any therapist recommendations you save — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on the device you are using.

This means:

- Your data never leaves your device.
- Your data is not transmitted over the internet.
- We cannot access, read, or recover your data.
- If you clear your browser data or use the "Delete all my data" option in Settings, your data is permanently removed.
- If you switch devices or browsers, your data does not transfer unless you manually export it and import it on the new device.

## Health and Sensitive Information

Compass is a mental health journaling tool. You may enter information about your emotional state, mental health patterns, therapy notes, and other sensitive topics. This information is health-related and may be protected under federal and state privacy laws.

Because Compass stores all data locally on your device and never transmits it to any server or third party:

- We do not collect, process, or store your health data.
- Your health data remains under your sole control on your device.
- No entity associated with Compass has access to your health information.

## Journal Entries Are Permanent

Once you save a journal entry, it cannot be edited or deleted through the app. This is a deliberate safety decision. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, preserving your entries ensures that a therapist, doctor, or loved one reviewing your history can see an accurate record of what you were experiencing. You can still delete all data by clearing your browser storage or using the reset option in Settings, but individual entries cannot be selectively removed.

## Your Privacy Rights

Many U.S. states grant residents specific rights regarding their personal data. Because Compass does not collect, process, or store any personal data, all of the following rights are automatically satisfied — but we list them here for transparency.

**Your rights under various state privacy laws include:**

- **Right to know/access:** You have the right to know what personal data is being collected about you. Compass does not collect any personal data.

- **Right to delete:** You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. You can delete all Compass data at any time by using the "Delete all my data" option in Settings or by clearing your browser's local storage.

- **Right to correct:** You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data. Compass does not collect personal data. Journal entries are intentionally immutable for safety reasons (see above).

- **Right to opt out of sale or sharing:** You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. Compass does not sell or share any data.

- **Right to limit processing of sensitive data:** You have the right to limit how sensitive data is processed. Compass does not process your data on any server. All processing happens locally on your device.

- **Right to data portability:** You have the right to receive your data in a portable format. You can export all your data as a JSON file at any time using the Export option in Settings.

- **Right to appeal:** If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you have the right to appeal. Since Compass does not collect data, there is nothing to appeal — but you may contact us with any concerns.

**These rights apply under the following state laws, among others:**

- California: California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
- Virginia: Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA)
- Colorado: Colorado Privacy Act (CPA)
- Connecticut: Connecticut Data Privacy Act (CTDPA)
- Utah: Utah Consumer Privacy Act (UCPA)
- Texas: Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA)
- Washington: My Health My Data Act (MHMDA)
- Oregon: Oregon Consumer Privacy Act (OCPA)
- Montana: Montana Consumer Data Privacy Act (MCDPA)
- Iowa: Consumer Data Protection Act
- Delaware: Personal Data Privacy Act (DPDPA)
- New Jersey: New Jersey Data Privacy Act (NJDPA)
- New Hampshire: New Hampshire Privacy Act
- Tennessee: Tennessee Information Protection Act (TIPA)
- Indiana: Indiana Consumer Data Protection Act (INCDPA)
- Kentucky: Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act (KCDPA)
- Minnesota: Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act
- Nebraska: Nebraska Data Privacy Act
- Rhode Island: Rhode Island Personal Data and Online Privacy Protection Act
- Florida: Florida Digital Bill of Rights (FDBR)

If your state has a privacy law not listed here, the same principles apply: Compass does not collect, process, or store your personal data, so your rights under that law are satisfied by default.

## Children's Privacy

Compass is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable under state law). Compass does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because no data is collected from anyone — adult or child — there is no children's data to protect. If you believe a child has used Compass, be aware that any data entered exists only on that child's device and can be removed by clearing browser storage.

## Data Breaches

Because Compass does not collect or store data on any server, there is no risk of a data breach in the traditional sense. Your data exists only on your device. The security of your data depends on the security of your device and browser. We recommend using a device passcode or biometric lock to protect access to your device.

## International Users

Compass is designed to comply with U.S. privacy laws. If you are accessing Compass from outside the United States, please be aware that your data is stored locally on your device under the same principles described above. We do not transfer data internationally because we do not transfer data at all.

For users in the European Union or United Kingdom, note that Compass does not process personal data subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR, as no personal data is collected or processed by us. Your data remains on your device under your control.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever add features that change how Compass handles data — for example, cloud sync, account creation, or third-party integrations — we will update this privacy policy and notify users within the app before any such features are activated. Any changes will be effective immediately upon publication of the updated policy.

We will never silently change our data practices. If Compass ever stops being a fully local, no-server application, we will make that clear.

## How to Delete Your Data

You have three options:

1. **In the app:** Go to Settings → "Delete all my data." This permanently removes all assessment answers, journal entries, therapist recommendations, and cached data from your device.

2. **Through your browser:** Clear your browser's site data or local storage for the Compass website. This removes everything.

3. **By uninstalling:** If you have installed Compass as a progressive web app, removing the app from your device will clear its stored data.

**Note:** Once data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. We recommend using the Export option in Settings to create a backup before deleting if you want to keep a copy of your entries.
